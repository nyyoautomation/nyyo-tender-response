On-premises option — zero access to your data Option

These points apply only to the on-premises version installed at your office, not to the online default.

Zero access to your data

Bids, prices and past files stay on your PC or mini-PC. The tool has no remote-access feature.

Built to run offline

Local AI included (Ollama + Mistral / Qwen). An internet connection is only needed to download the tool and its updates.

Checksum-verified installer

Checksum-verified installer (SHA-256). Source code available for your IT to review, and they can verify the file. OS code-signing provided with the delivered build.

You pull updates

Download updates yourself, when you want. Nothing installs silently in the background.

Plug & play mini-PC

Or a preconfigured mini-PC shipped to your office: plug in power and network, open the desktop icon. No remote tunnel.

Install while you watch

By video call (you share screen) or on site. Your IT can sit in and verify the installer checksum.

Honest limits

  • Hallucination risk — AI can invent a compliance claim; citations reduce risk, they don’t remove it.
  • Human review is mandatory — site leads and bid managers sign off before submission.
  • No automatic pricing math — bill-of-quantities prices come from you, not the model.
  • No guaranteed score — we structure to the published criteria; we don’t predict the jury.
  • Responsibility stays with the bidder — Nyyo Automation does not submit bids on your behalf.
  • This demo — the sample package is pre-analysed (extraction results are stored, not computed live); search, form, assembly and DOCX export run live in your browser on fictional data.
