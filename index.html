Frequently asked questions

Does the tool replace the person who prepares bids?

No. It prepares a draft: tender analysis, Go / No-Go, compliance matrix, technical proposal. Your team reviews, completes and decides. The bidder remains solely responsible for what is submitted.

Where do the proposal answers come from?

From your own documents: past proposals, references, CVs, certificates, plant lists. Each item cites its file and page. When no evidence is found, the row stays “Needs review” instead of being invented. The AI can be wrong: sources reduce that risk without removing it, hence the mandatory review.

Does the tool calculate my prices?

No. Price-schedule figures come from you. The tool reuses the amount you enter and never prices anything for you.

How does the Go / No-Go work?

The grid cross-checks the tender with your documents: time to deadline, similar references, key team, programme, certificates and insurance, plant, watch-outs and mandatory forms. It gives an indicative recommendation; the decision stays yours.

Where is my data stored?

Online, each company has its own isolated workspace, the AI runs on isolated infrastructure and your data is not used to train any model. Access is limited and logged, and you can request deletion. A confidentiality agreement is signed before you share a real document. A fully on-premises version is available as an option.

Do I need to install anything?

Not for the online version: everything happens in the browser. The version installed on your PC or an office mini-PC comes with the Guided plan.

Which documents can it read?

The usual tender documents in PDF, Word and Excel: rules, admin specs, technical specs, price schedule and appendices. This page uses a pre-analysed fictional tender so you can try everything without sending anything.

How much does it cost?

Three plans: Essential (2 tenders a month), Pro (4 a month) and Guided (8 a month, with import of your past proposals and a Word template in your colours). Pricing on quote; no setup fee on Essential and Pro.

How do I get started?

Try the demo on this page, then message us on LaborX with the kind of tenders you bid on. We open your workspace and help you load your first documents, over a video call where you share your screen.

Honest limits

  • Hallucination risk — AI can invent a compliance claim; citations reduce risk, they don’t remove it.
  • Human review is mandatory — site leads and bid managers sign off before submission.
  • No automatic pricing math — bill-of-quantities prices come from you, not the model.
  • No guaranteed score — we structure to the published criteria; we don’t predict the jury.
  • Responsibility stays with the bidder — Nyyo Automation does not submit bids on your behalf.
  • This demo — the sample package is pre-analysed (extraction results are stored, not computed live); search, form, assembly and DOCX export run live in your browser on fictional data.
  • Not available yet: tender monitoring, automatic filling of tender forms, team collaboration on one bid (tasks, statuses), buyer-imposed Excel response grids. We say so rather than let you assume otherwise.
